# OSTRAM LAC

OSTRAM LAC (OSeMOSYS for Transmission Modelling, Latin America and the Caribbean) is an optimisation model of the regional electricity system, with an emphasis on transmission, developed by Climate Lead Group. It covers 19 countries of Latin America and the Caribbean, close to 90% of the installed capacity and generation of the region, over 2023–2050. For each year, the model chooses the combination of investment and dispatch that minimises the total cost of the system (investment in generation, transmission and storage, operation and maintenance, and fuel) while supplying the demand of each country and sustaining a reserve margin. It is used to quantify how much it costs the region not to invest in transmission or, put another way, the benefit of investing in time in electricity transmission grids. This site documents both the **study** (context, methodology, scenarios, results) and the **tool** (installation, pipeline, configuration, data).

Source code: <https://github.com/Climate-Lead-Group/OSTRAM-LAC>
